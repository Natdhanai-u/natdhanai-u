# Welcome to my GitHub profile

My name is **Natdhanai Uaanant**. I am a high school student based in Bangkok, Thailand, with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Computer Engineering. 

I've always been curious about how things work, particularly computers and the technology we use in our everyday lives. As I started learning more about programming, I realized that what grabs my attention the most isn't just writing code, but understanding how everything works behind it. I always ask questions and seek the most accurate answers possible to connect things I already know with the things I've just learned.  

I enjoy visualizing and explaining what I'm learning by writing things down, breaking down complicated concepts into simpler ideas, and connecting what I already know with the things I am learning to expand and deepen my understanding. I believe that explaining concepts to another person or to myself is one of the best ways to understand those concepts more deeply.
